# Owner test: preparing ordinary conversation follow-ups

The October 22 owner direction authorizes faster ordinary replies and follow-up questions in the separate synthetic owner copy, with Heart, Dad's setup and the no-API-billing boundary preserved. This is a further S01 engineering experiment. It does not authorize a client rollout or complete person/device/clinical/genuine-day/full-slice acceptance.

## Selected behavior and boundaries

The default-off private `chat_mode: ahead-v1` requires the entire existing Sol-low/prepared/Heart/quick-read/spoken/ahead-meal/dialogue owner selection, with exact owner, synthetic participant and origin matching. Main never reads it. The test label becomes “Sol / low (prepared conversation + meals, Heart)”. The selected model/effort remain `gpt-6-sol`/`low`; Dad keeps Sol/high. Heart remains `af_heart`, normal speed, the pinned fp32 checkpoint and the previous twelve-thread/72-character test configuration. The locked Current client JavaScript/CSS are retained.

After a general, read-only model explanation, authenticated foreground audio retrieval can prepare three possible continuations: why, more detail and a simpler explanation. This uses the same single Nancy agent, bound owner Pro account and existing no-tool request path. It is not a new model, provider, recurring scheduler, background listener or external connector. A text-only reply does not trigger preparation.

Preparation starts after the preceding first audio is available; additional synthesis waits until its last audio part has been prepared. This gives current speech priority. Only exact whole-message continuations can use the result. Repetition returns the actual preceding reply and its first audio rather than asking the model to reconstruct it. An exact generic example request, a cold preparation miss or an early follow-up retains the short read-only conversation path when its preceding context still qualifies. New questions and facts keep normal reasoning and the complete care workflows where needed. No filler, tentative model stream or unvalidated speech is released to appear faster.

Each draft is memory-only, conversation/login-specific and valid for two minutes. Binding, fresh profile/preferences/handoffs/focus, local date, source hashes and actual history must still match. New scope, changed facts, unsupported intent, interruption, logout and end invalidate it. Questions about private facts, care, current events and clinical/action topics are excluded from preparation. A model suggestion never accepts a plan, records an activity, changes a protocol or contacts anyone. Human/tool text remains lower-trust data.

The model must return exactly the three named fields. Each usable candidate must be a bounded safe plain string; invalid candidates become null independently, while malformed/missing/extra JSON fields reject the whole response. Medical/private-resource language and claims that Nancy performed an action remain excluded. Ordinary scientific verbs such as sending airborne particles, and descriptive ingredients of a scent, are not care commands. If no suitable candidate exists, the next question receives a fresh reply. There is no inference retry, hidden model switch or API-key fallback.

There is one preparation job at a time, a twenty-second timeout, six attempts per conversation and eighteen per process, including failed or cancelled attempts. Duplicate audio fetches do not start additional jobs. An early repeat preserves its audio without starting a replacement preparation. These are bounded review settings; requests remain available after preparation is exhausted, with normal reasoning delay. Unused preparation can consume extra Pro allowance, and its requests/usage are counted separately from zero-model follow-up hits.

## Measurements and rejected approaches

Thirty-six direct synthetic answers compare the current policy, a compact policy at low effort, and the compact policy at none effort. Two reversed-order repetitions cover rain and music/memory chains, six questions per repetition/profile, using the same existing owner Pro route and native Heart. Median first validated audio is 2.880s current, 3.127s compact-low and 3.181s compact-none; worst observed values are 3.393s, 4.622s and 5.281s respectively. All reported reasoning-token counts are zero in this particular screen. Neither alternative is selected. This screen does not show that arbitrary new questions can be made consistently fast by lowering effort or shortening the policy.

An initial native pilot produces a 0.154s prepared why reply after 9.800s simulated playback. Its preparation takes 7.395s. The subsequent twenty-four-answer comparison exposes a reliability problem: five preparation requests fail validation and the candidate's follow-ups use fresh reasoning instead. This configuration is rejected despite the earlier successful pilot. The completed comparison and its consumption remain in the evidence; no result is silently replaced.

An intermediate diagnostic reaches ready state with only one of three candidates and still needs fresh reasoning for why (4.330s). Its privately captured response shows valid scientific uses of “send” and “ingredients” rejected by an overbroad lexical guard. Final validation accepts all three captured candidates without another provider request. The final change also retains qualifying context when an in-flight preparation is cancelled by an early follow-up, and prevents repeats from starting redundant preparation.

The model-only screen and initial native comparison are distinct populations and revisions, not final performance claims. ## Accuracy and remaining limits

The fixed synthetic review checks answer relevance, continuity, meaningful explanations, unknown private facts, no care/action claims and the tested general science. [NASA's raindrop explanation](https://gpm.nasa.gov/science/anatomy-of-a-raindrop) and [MIT's rain aerosol study](https://news.mit.edu/2015/rainfall-can-release-aerosols-0114) support the scoped rain explanations. [Janata's primary music-memory study](https://pscresearch.faculty.ucdavis.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/112/2014/11/Janata-music-evoked-memories.pdf) supports the general cue/emotion/memory relationship, not individual diagnosis or a treatment guarantee. One initial baseline explanation about gentle rain avoiding “washing away” scents is not established by the fetched mechanism source and is not certified as accurate. No general accuracy rate is inferred from this small population.

The [official OpenAI latency guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latency-optimization) supports measuring complete latency and reducing unnecessary requests/work. It does not establish this Pro route's entitlement or actual timings. Those are separately tested here. The [Sol documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-sol) lists low and none support; the measured comparison, rather than that support alone, determines the retained setting.

New topics and private/current/clinical/care questions still need fresh reasoning, and provider waits remain variable. Preparation itself takes seconds and may not finish before an early question. Short read-only routing avoids the larger workflow in that case but does not remove model latency. Audio-readiness measurements exclude actual microphone capture, endpointing, Safari transport, audible onset and subjective listening. Preparation and simulated playback are reported separately. No locked-screen/closed-browser voice, automatic microphone access, personal clinical suitability or full-slice acceptance is claimed.

## Review and rollback

Use the existing independent test site on port 10000 and the unchanged tester login. Ask for an ordinary explanation, let Nancy finish speaking, then try “Why does that happen?”, “Tell me more”, “Explain that more simply” or “Repeat that”. A new or more specific question receives a fresh answer. The site remains usable when preparation is unavailable or capped.

Before activation, the preceding private selection and entry are preserved. The dated rollback restores the prior dialogue/meal-follow-up trial and restarts only the identity-verified test child; it does not restore care data or credentials or stop the main app, supervisors or PostgreSQL. The rollback is prepared and guarded; it is not claimed to have been executed during this experiment.
