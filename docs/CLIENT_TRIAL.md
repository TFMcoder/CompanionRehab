1. Open My Day on the selected iPhone in Safari and sign in with the assigned account. **Talk to Nancy** stays available from each client view.
2. The authorized owner enters genuine tasks, meal choices and any local appointment through the minimal private setup, with the participant's preferences and time zone. Enter urgency and a date or time only when known. Leave an unknown time unscheduled. If there are no appointments, leave the list empty. Review these facts in My Day before planning.
3. Ask Nancy what matters now. Review the proposed tasks and meals, then accept only the plan you actually want. A saved plan records an intention; it does not record that anything was done or eaten.
4. Open **Tasks** and **Meals** to check the simple names and known times. Open **Activity** to see actual reports. The views should agree with Nancy's spoken readback.
5. During an ordinary day, report a task when completed, a meal when eaten and an appointment when attended. For food, distinguish a full meal, snack or unknown kind and give the actual time when known. Defer or reschedule a real pending item when appropriate. If a report is wrong, correct or void it and confirm that the history remains understandable.
6. Ask an ordinary nutrition or meal question. Nancy should identify the repository reference and its limits, respect supplied preferences and avoid treating a recipe as an approved meal or clinical instruction.

The participant may use buttons or, after route qualification, voice. The implemented rhythm prefers 06:00–09:00 for the morning routine with optional catch-up until 11:00, evening preparation around 16:00 and a soft 20:00 finish for discretionary tasks. Actual reports determine what is relevant: a recent full meal suppresses another automatic full-meal suggestion, and missing reports invite a question rather than a claim that no food was eaten. Conversation remains available at any hour. Foreground **Hey Nancy** is optional and needs separate consent on the actual device. A closed browser or locked phone is not promised to listen or speak.

Use **What Nancy should remember** in My Day to review an ordinary preference for future days or today only. Voice offers the same review before saving. Correct or remove it when needed; a daily choice expires from current context at the participant's local midnight. Preferences do not change approved care instructions. In **Day review**, a significant event can be recorded with its occurrence time and later corrected, reviewed or resolved. Record one only when it naturally exists; ordinary dialogue and unfinished tasks are not automatically converted into permanent memories or distress labels.

Quiet morning/startup/open preparation only refreshes local readiness; it neither speaks nor calls the model. If the participant chooses reminders, enable a small useful food-check window first, then choose a focus interval for an explicitly started ordinary task. Start, pause, resume and finish are reviewed actions. Finishing a task session stops its focus reminder; it does not mark the task completed. A reminder may be snoozed or declined without recording food or task completion. Do not delay eating, invent an incident or work for hours to manufacture a test. Intended-device prompt delivery and three genuine dates remain acceptance work.

In **Day review → Food timing choices**, the client can review and save personal spacing for automatic full-meal suggestions and the interval used by an opted-in food-report check. These choices survive reload and apply to actual occurrence times. They are ordinary planning preferences, not clinical thresholds, and do not turn on reminders or limit when the client may eat or talk. Equivalent bounded Nancy review is available only after that client's reasoning route is qualified. Activity includes the meal slot in names and controls when the same food appears in several meals.

If a save is unconfirmed, check the retained result or retry the exact reviewed command. Do not report the same meal again merely because speech or a network response was lost. The server retains the original receipt and command until the saved result reaches the client. Unknown results can be cancelled for fresh review; an already committed result is shown as saved.
