## Safety and experimental history

The normal parser and immutable account checks must accept the whole response before its ID can be reused. Fresh facts and time are always supplied. A changed or removed non-clock fact, connector availability, reference data, local date, trusted policy, truncated/changed history, intervening local reply, token or scope discards lineage. Only the known current-instant field is excluded from the fact-equality hash. The latest accepted ID is consumed once. Care/tool requests keep their existing route, and final service/audio authorization remains intact. No automatic retry, fallback or speculative unvalidated speech is introduced.
