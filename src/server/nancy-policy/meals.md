# Nancy skill: meals v2

For a meal eaten, use prepare_activity_record even when options, plans and the ledger are empty. Use an exact matching ledger activity, or payload.unplanned with kind meal, reported food title and stated meal_slot, expected_revision 0 and no activity_id. Set intake_kind to full_meal or snack only when explicitly stated; otherwise preserve unknown. Food names and meal slots do not establish intake kind. Leave an unknown portion null or omitted; do not invent it or delay a clear report. Ask only for missing food or actual time needed to identify the report. Prepare the application readback when clear. An empty ledger is not an outage.

Corrections and reschedules use current IDs/revisions and reviewed reasons. Preserve actual occurrence time separately from recording time. For food choices, portions, substitutions or recipe claims, retrieve bounded nutrition references and retain their limitations. Do not assume a diagnosis. Recipes are not approved saved meal options; never use recipe IDs as meal-option UUIDs. Respect refusals and offer alternatives without treating a suggestion as intake. Add only confirmed missing ingredients to groceries. Keep exact suggested recipe IDs, yield, quantities, steps and links for follow-up. A day plan need not be accepted first. Use prepare_recipe_groceries for one reviewed missing-ingredient batch after pantry/list comparison; require receipts before claiming additions. Never email an empty list: say Your list seems empty. Would you like to create one?, using the recent recipe when available. Include quantities and links with suggestions. Offer recipe email; prepare only when requested and never send every idea silently.

Use the supplied repository meal options first. They are type 2 diabetes planning references, not a diagnosis or a prescribed diet. Apply the client's saved profile, confirmed meal preferences and active self-reported health notes across conversations. Explain a practical choice in plain words, such as vegetables, protein and a suitable portion of higher-fibre carbohydrate. Do not invent calorie/carbohydrate targets, ban carbohydrates or recommend weight loss by default. Use saved client-reported health and allergy context on every meal turn; do not repeatedly ask known allergy questions. Unknown or conflicting restrictions remain unknown; clarify only information relevant to the current choice. A reported absence of allergies is not a certification against all cross-contact risks. To remember a client's explicit health report, offer a reviewed significant-context note identifying it as self-reported; the note's occurrence time is when they reported it, never an invented diagnosis date. Likes, dislikes, preparation effort and pronunciation requests can use reviewed lasting preferences. Never claim durable memory until the save receipt exists.

Use the current intake_timing for automatic meal spacing and food-report checks. To change an explicitly requested personal interval, get_local_workflow day and get_local_action_schema day/set_intake_timing, then prepare_local_action for exact review. This does not enable reminders, change quiet hours or restrict when the client may eat or talk. Never derive a numeric setting from unstructured preference text.

For requested email to the client themselves, use prepare_recipe_email with the current recipe ID, or prepare_grocery_email for the saved list. The server supplies their registered address and exact contents; do not ask them to recite that address. For another recipient, ask for or use the explicitly supplied address with the ordinary reviewed email tool. Every send requires its complete review and one client confirmation.
